# Round 2 conservative rework of GaganEA — trailing-distance follow-up (v2.11) (v2)

This pass re-reviews the Round 2 rework after the follow-up that addressed v1's three non-blocking concerns. The v1 blocker-adjacent concern — that the newly-live real-SL trails sat at $0.30 and $0.15 on gold, far inside tick noise — has been fixed by raising the default input literals rather than changing the unit math: `Trail_Step_Pips` 30→400, `Basket_Lock_Pips` 30→500, `Basket_Trail_Step` 15→250, `Master_Lock_Pips` 30→500, `Master_Trail_Step` 15→250. The `* _Point` unit is kept, which is the right call: it stays consistent with `StopLoss_Pips`/`T1_Pips`, which are also raw points on 3-digit gold. The trailing functions and their monotonic guards are byte-for-byte unchanged from v1 — only literals moved — and the two other v1 concerns were deliberately kept with written justifications added to the log.

**Watch for:** nothing blocking. The new distances are internally consistent (Lock ≥ Trail_Step on both basket and master paths, so each trail arms with a positive locked floor and ratchets sensibly) (confirmed); the master trail still fires off a summed, not per-position, profit metric, now documented as an accepted trade-off bounded by the 2-position cap (confirmed, accepted); no MQL5 compiler/MT5 in this sandbox, so this is a read-through only (confirmed).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The fix is a pure default-literal change. The trail unit stays `* _Point` — correct, because on 3-digit gold the EA treats every `*_Pips` input as raw points, and `StopLoss_Pips`/`T1_Pips` already do the same, so switching only the trails to a `pip` (point×10) scale would have made them the odd ones out. Raising the literals instead keeps one consistent convention across the whole SL/target/trail family.

The new numbers hold together arithmetically. Both the basket and master paths arm when summed profit reaches `Lock_Pips` (500 pts, ~$5) and set the give-back floor at `Lock − Trail_Step` (500 − 250 = 250 pts, ~$2.50); because Lock ≥ Trail_Step the floor is positive, so the trail locks real profit the moment it arms and then ratchets up in 250-pt steps. The individual after-T2 trail at 400 pts (~$4) sits sensibly between the ~500-pt T1 and the 1000-pt SL: it only engages after T2 (1000 pts of gain), so it locks in roughly $6 of a $10 move and trails wider than the ~$4 EMA-distance noise band.

No logic beyond the literals changed. `ApplyTrailingToTickets`, `ApplyMasterTrailing`, and `ApplyBreakevenToTickets` are identical to what v1 reviewed, guards included, so the safety properties v1 established (a stop only ever ratchets toward profit, never backward) still hold. The two v1 concerns left open — the master trail keying off an aggregate profit figure, and the full 1000-pt stop being exposed until T1 — are now recorded in the log as deliberate trade-offs with reasoning, which is the right disposition for behavioral judgments that only a backtest can settle.

### Trailing-distance fix: literals up, unit unchanged

The one substantive change since v1 is five default values. The trails still compute `profitTarget * _Point`, so on 3-digit XAUUSD a value of N means N points ≈ $N/100. v1 flagged that 30 and 15 in that unit produced $0.30 and $0.15 trails; the follow-up did not touch the unit (which would have desynced these from `StopLoss_Pips=1000` and `T1_Pips=500`, also raw points) and instead moved the literals to 400/500/250. This is the correct resolution — one unit convention across the whole family — and the log's "trailing distances widened for gold" entry states exactly this rationale.

### Arming and ratchet relationship holds on both paths

Both `ManageBasketTrailing` and `CheckMasterEquityProtection` follow the same shape: arm when `profitPips >= Lock_Pips`, seed the trail floor at `profitPips - Trail_Step`, then raise the floor as profit makes new highs and close the side when profit falls back to the floor. With Lock=500 and Trail_Step=250, arming happens at ~$5 of summed profit and the initial floor sits at ~$2.50 — a positive locked amount, not underwater. Had Trail_Step exceeded Lock the seeded floor would have been negative and the trail would have armed already in a giving-back state; that inversion does not occur here. The individual after-T2 trail (400 pts) is guarded by `newSL > op` for buys / `newSL < op` for sells, so it can only ever sit in profit territory, and at 400 pts against a position already 1000 pts in the money it locks ~600 pts.

### Guards and wiring unchanged from v1

The diff for `ApplyTrailingToTickets`, `ApplyMasterTrailing`, and `ApplyBreakevenToTickets` is empty relative to v1 (guards intact: `newSL > sl && newSL > op` for BUY; `(sl == 0 || newSL < sl) && newSL < op` for SELL), so the prior safety analysis carries over unmodified. The two accepted concerns are behavioral, not correctness: the master trigger derives from `buyProfitPips`/`sellProfitPips` summed across same-side tickets, and breakeven engages only after T1, leaving the full 1000-pt stop live before it. Both are now written up in the log as intentional — the master-trail note correctly observing that the per-ticket modify is per-position correct even though its trigger is aggregate.

### Log accuracy and scope

The Round 2 log section records the exact new numbers (400/500/250/500/250) and their rationale, marks all metrics "pending next backtest" without inventing results, and adds the two justification paragraphs for the accepted concerns. Scope and consistency hold: only `GaganEA.mq5` and `ITERATION_LOG.md` are touched; `grep 2.10` in the source returns nothing (the only `2.10` hits are legitimate historical references in the log's Round 0/1 sections); braces and parens balance 172/172 and 896/896; and no new non-ASCII bytes were added (the two em-dash comment lines are pre-existing and unmodified).
